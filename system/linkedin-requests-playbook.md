# LinkedIn "Looking for an agency" Playbook

Zaya's most-used outreach channel: people posting on LinkedIn that they need a social media /
content / creative agency. They're warm leads with a stated need, so speed and relevance matter
more than clever openers.

Search engines barely index fresh LinkedIn posts, so the **founder finds the posts** and the
system drafts the replies from screenshots.

---

## 1. Finding posts (founder, ~10 min/day)

LinkedIn search → **Posts** tab → filters: **Date posted: Past 24 hours** (or Past week),
**Sort by: Latest**. Run these one at a time:

- `"looking for a social media agency"`
- `"looking for social media agency"`
- `"social media agency" requirement`
- `"looking for a content creation agency"`
- `"looking for a creative agency"`
- `"agency requirement" creatives`
- `"reels" "agency" "DM me"`
- `"social media manager or agency"`
- `"hiring" "social media agency"`
- `"looking for a video production agency"`
- `"OOH" agency requirement` (Block proof is strongest here)

Add a location if useful: `Mumbai`, `Delhi`, `Bangalore`, `Dubai`, `Gujarat`.

**Skip** posts that:
- are older than ~2 weeks, unless they're specific and high-value
- come from agencies reselling (they want cheap white-label work)
- are pure "influencer" or "performance marketing only" requests
- ask for a full-time employee only, not an agency

---

## 2. Picking the reply format

| What the post says | First move | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Gives an email; asks for portfolio / case studies | Email | §3B |
| "DM me" / no email; you're not connected | Connection note (≤200 chars) | §3A |
| They reply with their email | Intro email | §3C |
| You have their phone number | WhatsApp | §3D |
| Asks for pricing / approach | Email with approach + pricing | §3B + pricing line |

Always mirror **their** list of needs, in their words, and give one proof point per need.

## 4. Proof picker (quick version of proof-library.md)

| Their need / industry | Lead with |
|---|---|
| OOH, metro, billboards, agencies | Block Agency rebrand + brand film |
| LinkedIn content, B2B | Block's LinkedIn/Instagram post system |
| Fintech, apps, tech | Aspora ad film, Groww films/YouTube, Captain Fresh motion |
| Product launch, D2C, FMCG, grooming | Sensi Flo 4 launch film (via partner agency) |
| Events | Groww hackathon film |
| Apparel, fashion | Naga film for Ahankarwear |
| Issue-led, NGO, public affairs | Equitas *Human Rights* film |
| Luxury, hospitality, 3D, performance ads | No direct proof; lead with their need + the site |

Never claim results, OOH ads for Block, or experience in a category the library doesn't cover.

---

## 5. After sending

Add the lead to `leads/master.csv` (Prospect Type, Contact Method, Status `Contacted` once
sent), and save the draft under `outreach/<channel>/<slug>.md`. Follow up once after ~4–5
days if there's no reply; LinkedIn requests go stale fast.
