# Learnings

What we've learned about which prospects, angles and channels work. The goal is to
make each round of outreach sharper than the last.

Updated by `/learn` (future command) or manually. **Only record what the data
shows.** Mark small samples as such.

---

## 1. What to track

Derived from `leads/master.csv` and `leads/rejected/`:

- Reply rate and positive-reply rate, by:
  - Lane (A brands vs B agencies)
  - Industry / agency type
  - Market
  - Channel (Instagram / LinkedIn / email)
  - COI tier (does Tier A actually outperform B?)
  - Proof used vs not used; proof in the first message vs after the reply
  - Angle type (e.g. launch-based, video gap, motion gap, 3D product, OOH/DOOH)
- Follow-up effectiveness (replies after FU1 / FU2)
- Most common rejection reasons (from `leads/rejected/`)
- Time from first contact to reply

## 2. Starting hypotheses (untested)

These are **assumptions to test**, not findings.

1. Lane B OOH/advertising agencies will reply at a higher rate when the Block Agency
   proof is referenced in the first message.
2. Lane A prospects with a recent, verifiable trigger (launch, rebrand, expansion)
   will outperform those without one.
3. A single sharp observation will outperform messages with multiple points.
4. Instagram DMs will work better for founder-led brands; LinkedIn and email for
   agencies and larger companies.
5. The UAE may respond better early on, because the Dubai-based proof is locally
   relevant.
