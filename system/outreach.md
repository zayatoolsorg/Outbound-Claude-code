# Outreach — Philosophy, Tone and Channel Rules

All outreach is **drafted** by the system and **reviewed and sent by the founder**.
Nothing is ever sent automatically.

---

## 1. Philosophy

The message should read as if a creative person noticed something specific.

> "I noticed something interesting about your brand."
> not
> "I want to sell you something."

- The first message shows genuine research: one specific, accurate observation.
- It **starts a conversation**. It doesn't close a deal or book a call.
- Keep it short. Don't paste the audit into the first message. Hold it back for
  when they reply.
- One idea per message.
- Observations about their work must be true, specific and respectful. Name a gap
  as an opportunity, never as a criticism.
- Never state an inference as fact ("your videos aren't converting"). Frame it as a
  thought ("felt like there's room for...").

## 2. Tone

**Be:** confident, human, observant, short, premium, intelligent, direct.

**Never be:** desperate, overly enthusiastic, corporate, generic, cringe, salesy.

## 3. Banned phrases and patterns

- "Hope you're doing well" / "Hope this finds you well"
- "We are a leading agency" / "award-winning" (unless verified)
- "We help brands grow" / "take your brand to the next level"
- "We would love to collaborate" / "love to work with you"
- "Let's jump on a quick call" / "15 minutes of your time"
- "Would you be interested in our services?"
- "Your brand is amazing" / empty compliments
- "I came across your profile"
- Lists of services in the first message
- Exclamation marks in the first message; emojis in LinkedIn and email
- Fake familiarity, flattery, urgency or scarcity
- Anything that sounds like a template with the name swapped in

## 4. Message structure (all channels)

1. **Hook**: a specific observation about *them* (a campaign, a launch, a format, a
   visual choice).
2. **Thought**: a brief, intelligent point of view on the opportunity (framed as
   inference).
3. **Credibility (optional)**: one light line on who Zaya is, or a proof reference
   when `proof-library.md` rates it High for this prospect.
4. **Open door**: a low-pressure question or offer, e.g. to share a couple of
   thoughts or references, not a sales call.

## 5. Channel rules

### Instagram DM
- Conversational, like one creative talking to another.
- **~40–70 words.** 2–4 short lines.
- Reference a specific post, reel or series.
- No links in the first DM unless proof relevance is High and the link is confirmed.
- Send from Zaya's brand account (founder decides).

### LinkedIn
- Professional but human. Written to a named, verified person.
- **Connection note: ≤ 300 characters** (a single observation + reason to connect).
- **Message after connecting: ~60–100 words.**
- Anchor on their role or a recent company milestone (from a sourced fact).

### Email
- Slightly more developed, still concise: **~80–140 words**.
- **Subject line:** short, specific, lower-key (e.g. references their launch or
  campaign), no clickbait, no "Quick question".
- Only to publicly listed addresses or ones the founder provides. **Never guessed or
  pattern-generated emails.**
- Signature (all channels, whenever a sign-off is needed):
  ```
  Vedant Singhal
  Founder, Zaya Productions
  +91 98681 27751
  https://www.zayaproductions.com/
  ```
  Include the phone line in emails and WhatsApp; leave it out of LinkedIn notes.
- Messages written in the first person ("I run Zaya Productions") are from Vedant.
- A proof link only if it's confirmed in `proof-library.md`.

Don't write the same message for every platform. Each channel draft should read
natively.

## 6. Lane-specific angles

**Lane A — Brands:** lead with the gap between who they are and how they currently
look or move on screen. Suggest what could exist (a launch film, 3D product
visuals, a motion system, a higher-craft social format).

**Lane B — Agencies:** lead with their work and a specific execution area (motion,
3D, post, DOOH animation, social cut-downs). Position Zaya as a specialist partner
that extends their creative execution, never as cheap capacity. When the angle is
the agency's *own* brand or social presence (especially OOH/media agencies), the
Block Agency proof is often the strongest opener (competitors of Block may be
approached). When the angle is motion or film partnership, lead with a work
piece instead.

## 7. Follow-ups

- **Max two follow-ups** per channel, then mark as `Closed – no response`.
- **Follow-up 1:** ~5–7 days after first contact. Adds something new: a relevant
  reference, a sharper thought or a proof piece. Never "just bumping this".
- **Follow-up 2:** ~10–14 days after follow-up 1. Short, gracious, leaves the
  door open.
- Don't switch to a second channel for the same person without the founder's
  approval.
- If they reply "not now", set `Parked` with a revisit date, not a follow-up.
- Drafts go in `followups/<slug>.md`, with the due date mirrored in the
  `Follow-up Date` column of `master.csv`.

## 8. Pre-flight checklist (every draft)

- [ ] Every factual claim about the prospect is sourced in the research file
- [ ] No inference is phrased as fact
- [ ] No banned phrases
- [ ] Within the word limit for the channel
- [ ] Proof use matches `proof-library.md` (relevance + timing + allowed wording)
- [ ] No invented names, titles, emails or URLs
- [ ] Reads like a person, not a template
- [ ] Status set to `Drafted`, not `Contacted`
