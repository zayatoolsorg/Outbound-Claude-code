# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Who Zaya Productions targets, why, and what to look for. Read this before any
discovery or research task.

---

## 1. The core question

For **brands** the question is not "does this company need social media?"

It is:

> **Is there a noticeable gap between the quality or positioning of this company and
> the quality of its visual communication?**

That gap is the opportunity.

For **agencies** the question is:

> **Does this agency produce (or sell) work that needs premium motion, 3D, video,
> post-production or production capacity that a specialist partner could extend?**

A company with good content is not automatically a bad prospect. A sophisticated
brand can still have a specific project, launch, format or capability gap.

## 3. Lane A — Direct brands

### Industries (examples, not a closed list)

Luxury · Fashion · Beauty · Skincare · Jewellery · Automotive · Hospitality ·
Real estate · Food & beverage · Consumer technology · SaaS · Fintech ·
Entertainment · Sports · Travel · Architecture / interiors · Lifestyle ·
Premium services · other visually interesting businesses.

### What a strong Lane A prospect looks like

Some combination of:

- A valuable, visual or visually explainable product or service
- Premium or ambitious positioning
- A recognisable or growing brand
- Active marketing and social presence
- Evidence of paid advertising
- A visible creative gap (see §5)
- A need for more sophisticated video, motion, 3D, premium production or
  performance creative

### How Zaya is positioned to Lane A

A premium creative studio that sees a specific opportunity in how the brand
presents itself and can execute at the level the brand deserves.

---

## 4. Lane B — Agencies

### Agency types

OOH agencies · Advertising agencies · Creative agencies · Branding agencies ·
Experiential agencies · Social agencies · Marketing agencies · Production companies
(where a partnership makes sense rather than direct competition).

### What a strong Lane B prospect looks like

- Produces campaign work that needs motion, 3D, video, VFX, post or social cut-downs
- Visible volume of work (active portfolio, recent campaigns, OOH/DOOH work)
- Signs of capacity pressure: hiring motion/3D/video/editor roles, freelance calls,
  rapid growth, new offices or new big accounts (**only if publicly verifiable**)
- Strong strategy or design, but weaker motion or video execution in their portfolio
- Work across international clients or markets

### How Zaya is positioned to Lane B

A **specialist creative partner** that extends an agency's execution capability in
motion design, 3D, post-production, editing, VFX, social content and production,
at a premium standard.

**Never** position Zaya as cheap white-label labour, overflow outsourcing or a
low-cost production vendor.

### Proof relevance for Lane B

The Block Agency case study (Dubai-based global OOH/advertising agency) is usually
**highly relevant** for OOH agencies and **potentially highly relevant** for
advertising and creative agencies. See `system/proof-library.md`.

## 5. Signals to look for

Record each signal as a FACT with a source. Don't assume any of these.

**Momentum signals**
- Recent campaign launches, product launches, new collections
- Expansion into new markets or new locations
- Rebrand or brand refresh
- Funding announcement (verified source only)
- Hiring marketing, content, creative, motion, video or social roles
- Increasing social activity or new channels
- New marketing initiatives, partnerships, sponsorships
- OOH campaigns or visually ambitious campaigns
- Existing agency relationships (publicly credited)
- Active paid ads (e.g. visible in the Meta Ad Library)

**Creative-gap signals**
- Strong product or service, weak visual presentation
- Strong static design, weak video
- Repetitive short-form content; one format used over and over
- Generic, template-driven social formats
- Weak or absent motion design
- Weak product storytelling; features shown, not felt
- Inconsistent visual identity across website, social and ads
- Premium pricing or positioning, but mid-market visuals
- An obvious opportunity for high-production content (launch films, 3D product
  visuals, brand films, campaign motion)

---

## 6. Never assume (write `Not verified.`)

Revenue · Budget · Funding · Employee count · Marketing spend · Decision-maker
identity · Agency relationships · Campaign results.

---

## 7. Disqualifiers (reject or park)

- Company or its official channels can't be verified
- No meaningful marketing activity and no sign of investment in presentation
- Clearly unable to buy premium creative (e.g. sole trader, hobby business),
  **based on evidence, not assumptions**
- Business model conflicts with Zaya's positioning (e.g. only wants the cheapest
  high-volume content)
- Reputational, ethical or legal concerns
- Potential conflict with an existing Zaya client (e.g. a direct competitor of
  Block Agency): **flag it for the founder, don't decide alone**
- A production company that would compete directly rather than partner

---

## 8. Anti-patterns in prospecting

- Chasing volume: long lists with thin research
- Choosing companies only because they are famous
- Choosing companies only because their content is bad (they may not value creative)
- Treating "has Instagram" as a signal
- Assuming a decision-maker from a job title pattern
