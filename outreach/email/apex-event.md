Events have an advantage most brands don't: every event is three rounds of content.
- **Before:** teasers, speaker or artist reveals, countdowns and behind-the-scenes setup.
- **During:** live stories, crowd moments and quick reels while the energy is high.
- **After:** a recap film, highlights and testimonials that sell the next event.

Planned properly, one event becomes weeks of content, and the page never goes quiet between
events.
