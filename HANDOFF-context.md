## 3. Writing rules

- Tone: confident, human, observant, short, premium, direct. Never desperate, salesy, corporate or cringe.
- **Banned:** "Hope you're doing well", "We are a leading agency", "We help brands grow",
  "We would love to collaborate", "Let's jump on a quick call", "Would you be interested in our
  services?", "Your brand is amazing", empty flattery, lists of every service in a first DM.
- Mirror the prospect's own words and their list of needs; give one proof point per need.
- Critique gently: talk about the gap ("doesn't quite show that yet", "undersells"), never "your website is bad".
- Separate facts from assumptions. Don't state guesses about them as facts.
- **Length:** LinkedIn connection note ≤ 200 characters (count them). LinkedIn message ~60–100
  words. Instagram DM ~40–75 words. Email ~120–200 words. WhatsApp: one message, name first.
- End with a low-friction next step (a yes/no offer to send ideas, or "open to a short conversation?").
- Only use email addresses I give you or that are published in the prospect's post. Never guess.

## 4. Channel playbook (what works for me)

My best source: **LinkedIn posts saying "looking for a social media / creative agency"**. One
connection note that mirrored the post's list of needs got a reply with an email in ~2 minutes.

- Post gives an email + asks for portfolio → email mirroring their list, one proof per item.
- "DM me" / not connected → connection note ≤200 chars.
- They reply with email → short **intro email** (below).
- I have a phone number → WhatsApp.
- They ask for pricing → include a pricing line (I fill in the numbers).

**Intro email template (worked well):**
```
Subject: Introducing Zaya Productions

Hi {Name},

Thanks for sharing your email on LinkedIn. Here's a quick introduction.

Zaya Productions is a creative studio. We handle social media end to end: content planning,
reels, posts and stories, shoots, design, motion and editing, all produced in-house.

A few brands we've worked with:
- Block Agency, an independent OOH agency headquartered in Dubai. We did a full rebrand
  covering identity, website, Instagram and LinkedIn system, and brand film.
- Groww: films and YouTube content.
- Aspora and Captain Fresh: motion and ad films.
- Bombay Shaving Company: a launch film for Sensi Flo 4, made with a partner agency.

You can see the work here: https://www.zayaproductions.com/

Would love to learn more about {Company} and what you're looking for. Let me know a good time
to talk, or feel free to call me directly.

{signature}
```

**OOH-agency template (website + social, used for Talon and Excite):** open with "your work is
impressive, but your website and social don't quite show that yet"; two bold offers (Website
refresh / Social media management); then the Block paragraph ("Their work was on some of the
world's best screens, but their brand read like a media vendor. We designed and built a new
website around their work, and created a LinkedIn and Instagram feed built like a media plan…");
case study link; "Would you be open to a short conversation?"
