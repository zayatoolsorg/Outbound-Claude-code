## Facts (with sources)
- FACT: Independent creative and events agency in Dubai, female-owned and led. [https://campaignme.com/agency/jwi-dubai/]
- FACT: Founded 2000 by John Wilford; Dubai presence since 2015; acquired by long-standing MD Charli Wright in April 2025. [https://campaignme.com/independent-creative-agency-jwi-acquired-by-charli-wright/]
- FACT: February 2026: Adele Baxter (previously Client Services Director) promoted to Managing Director; Charli Wright became CEO. Baxter's mandate is to "further strengthen JWI's strategic partnerships and creative offerings". She was instrumental in the long-term Gillette (P&G) partnership. [https://campaignme.com/jwi-promotes-adele-baxter-to-managing-director-signalling-new-phase-of-growth/ · https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/people-in-the-news/jwis-new-managing-director-signals-next-phase-of-growth-jpgvfz6r]
- FACT: Gillette "Father's Day is Closer Than You Think" (with JWI) won Integrated Campaign of the Year at the WOW Awards 2025. The campaign was built from:
  - street interviews showing people didn't know when Father's Day was
  - nominations of father figures for experiences like a helicopter ride or a deep-sea dive, captured as content across TikTok, Instagram and radio
  - a three-day takeover of Dubai Festival City Mall, with photos shown on LED screens in real time

  Reported: 68M+ reach, 280K+ engagements, 3% engagement rate. [https://campaignme.com/gillette-gives-the-regions-dads-a-fathers-day-to-remember/]
- FACT: Benjamin (Ben) Thomas was promoted to Creative Director (reported July 2025). He joined in 2017 as the first in-house designer and grew the creative function from one person to a multidisciplinary team. His background is in branding, art/design direction and digital design. [https://campaignme.com/jwi-promotes-benjamin-thomas-to-creative-director/ · https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/2025/07/15/jwi-appoints-new-creative-director/]
- FACT: Other brands named in coverage: Braun, Bacardi, Patrón, Epson, Philips. [https://campaignme.com/tag/charli-wright/ (search summary; exact article not opened)]
- FACT (search summary; source page not opened): Meghan Hird is named as Head of Experiential; George Sharrock and Dana Harazallah are named in the creative and production team.
